HiFi is a modern, privacy-focused open source music client built with Compose Multiplatform for Android and Desktop.

Key Features:
- Ad-free streaming powered by YouTube Music
- Background audio playback with persistent media controls
- Synced and translated lyrics support (LRCLIB, Spotify, and YouTube Transcripts)
- Offline playback and smart audio caching
- Built-in 10-band equalizer and custom sound profiles
- High-speed browsing for Charts, Moods, Genres, and Podcasts
- Spotify Canvas video background support
- SponsorBlock and Return YouTube Dislike integration
- Multi-account YouTube Music sync and custom playlist management
- Android Auto integration with full queue and search support
- Sleep timer and customizable playback speed
- Modern Material You (Material 3 Expressive) interface with dynamic color theming

HiFi connects directly to public music backends without tracking, ads, or data collection.
